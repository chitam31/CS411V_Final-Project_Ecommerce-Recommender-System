A/B test: blending trending items lowers CTR

Design

A: ALS only · B: ALS + 30 % trending; stable hash of the user id

Traffic

350,160 simulated impressions over 7 days, position-dependent click model

p = 0.015

CTR 2.094 % (A) vs 1.978 % (B), z = −2.43: B is 5.5 % worse

Conversion

0.234 % vs 0.239 %, p = 0.75: no difference

Power

76,972 impressions per group needed — reached; B below A on all 7 days

Group A sees ALS only, group B ALS with thirty percent trending items. Real visitors are too few, so impressions are simulated with a position-dependent click model and labelled as simulated. Over 350 thousand impressions, B has a significantly lower click-through rate, p equals 0.015, and no difference in conversion. The conclusion holds only under the assumptions of the simulation, which gives trending items no bonus. The dashboard on the right also counts the 230 real web impressions logged on 11 November, which is why it reads p equals 0.0135 over eight days.
