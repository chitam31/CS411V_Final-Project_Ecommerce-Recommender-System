Five DAGs implement the workflows of the specification. DAG 1, on the right, is the nightly pipeline: it waits for new data, rebuilds silver and the matrix, tunes ALS, evaluates it and branches — new recommendations only if NDCG is not worse than the served model. The other four monitor the stream, compute the A/B test hourly, maintain the Delta tables and reproduce experiments.
