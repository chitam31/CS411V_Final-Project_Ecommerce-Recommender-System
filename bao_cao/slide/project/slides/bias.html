Coverage is limited by popularity bias

99.2 % of ALS slots fall in the 10 % most popular items. Coverage is 3.44 % against a structural ceiling of 75 % — the cause is bias, not the number of slots.

Coverage is the one threshold we did not meet with pure ALS: 3.44 percent against a target of 10. The specification accepts this if it is analysed. Ten recommendations for 9,467 test users give almost 95 thousand slots for 126 thousand items, so the ceiling is 75 percent. The real cause is popularity bias: items with few interactions get weak latent vectors and rarely reach a top ten.
