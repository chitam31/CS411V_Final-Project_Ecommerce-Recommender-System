Three cold-start strategies are pure functions shared by the batch job and the web application. A brand-new user sees the newest trending items; a user with fewer than five interactions sees best-sellers of the categories they viewed, interleaved with trending; and new items that ALS does not know enter through the last two slots. The screenshot is the live system: a user created with one click immediately gets the new-user strategy, and the page marks that user as outside the A/B experiment. Product names and pictures are illustrations, because RetailRocket is anonymised.
