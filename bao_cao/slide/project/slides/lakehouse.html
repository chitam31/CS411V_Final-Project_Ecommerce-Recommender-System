The lakehouse has two layers. The upper one is storage on HDFS with Delta Lake and has only three tiers: bronze keeps raw events exactly as received, silver keeps cleaned interactions, gold keeps every table that is served. The lower one is compute, made of Spark jobs: nap_su_kien moves events from Kafka into bronze; bronze_to_silver reads bronze, deduplicates by id and by content, filters bots and writes silver, with rejected rows going to _quarantine; chuan_bi_ma_tran reads silver and builds the feature matrix; ALS training, recommendation generation and blending follow, and ghi_gold MERGEs into gold. The two streaming jobs for trending and the impression log write straight into gold. Features and _quarantine are only intermediate tables of the compute layer, not a storage tier.
