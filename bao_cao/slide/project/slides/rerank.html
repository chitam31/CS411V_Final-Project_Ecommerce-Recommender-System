As a de-biasing method we re-rank 200 ALS candidates by normalised ALS score plus beta times normalised novelty. Up to beta equal one, accuracy and diversity improve together, because the test month contains many items that are rare in training. We deployed that configuration. Reaching ten percent needs candidate sources other than ALS, which is our main future work.
